# Security Policy

This policy applies to every repository in the AKRENON organisation.

## Reporting a vulnerability

Email **disclosure@akrenon.com**. Please do not open a public issue or pull request for a security problem.

Include what you can:

- the affected project and version or commit
- what the issue is and what an attacker could do with it
- steps or a proof of concept to reproduce it

## Encryption

Encrypt sensitive reports with PGP. The current key and its fingerprint are published at
https://akrenon.com/responsible-disclosure/#pgp-public-key and at https://akrenon.com/.well-known/pgp-key.txt.

## What happens next

- I acknowledge every report within 48 hours.
- I keep you informed while the fix is prepared.
- Disclosure is coordinated within a 90-day window from the first report. If a fix needs more time, I will tell you why and agree on a new date with you.

The full policy is at https://akrenon.com/responsible-disclosure/.
